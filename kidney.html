Kidney Project

One disciplined plan for supportive care.

This project consolidates approaches explored over time into one structured, measured plan, tracked over a year or more.

View Library About CUTDESIGN
Problem

Start with the need

Chronic kidney conditions rarely respond to any single treatment. Scattered, ad-hoc efforts are harder to learn from than one structured plan followed with discipline.

Approach

One year, one structured plan

Interventions explored to date are brought into a single tracked plan, with consistent measurement over roughly a year, so real progress can be told apart from noise.

Support

Backed through crowdfunding

This project is funded through crowdfunding. Supporters get visibility into the process and the measurement results, with the level of access matched to their level of support.
