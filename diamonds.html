Diamonds & Gemstones

Faceting design built on real cutting experience.

Cutdesign draws on hands-on diamond and gemstone faceting and polishing work, using an unusual 3D design approach that very few in the field use.

View Library About CUTDESIGN
Foundation

Second-generation cutter, since the 1990s

A cleaver and polisher building 3D models since the late 1990s — from hand-drawn sketches to computer models by 2001, using ray-trace and global-illumination light analysis to shape the design.

Approach

From shape to faceting order

Built on a principle set in the mid-1990s — every action performed on the polishing wheel can also be done on the computer. Upload a stone's shape, diameter, and dimensions, and the system plans a faceting sequence and delivers a ready 3D file.

Reach

Diamonds, gemstones, and CVD

Built to serve diamond and gemstone industries, jewelers, and end customers — including fancy color stones — with far more creative freedom now possible through CVD diamonds.
